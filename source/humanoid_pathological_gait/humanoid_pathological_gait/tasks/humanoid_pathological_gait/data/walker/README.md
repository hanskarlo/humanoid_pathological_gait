# Smart Walker asset

AgileX Ranger Mini v3 base with one transverse handlebar and an ATI Axia 6-axis F/T sensor joint:
the walker whose real admittance controller is `smart-walker-ws/src/sw_admittance_controller/`.
Staged 2026-09-24 for the walker evaluation (`docs/walker_evaluation_plan.md` in the outer repo).

| file | what it is |

## Measured on the faithful asset (pxr, no simulator)

- Total mass **62.3 kg**:
  - base 10 kg;
  - 4 steering links × 1 kg;
  - 4 wheels × 8 kg;
  - 2 pillars × 5 kg;
  - handle base 5 kg;
  - F/T link 0.2 kg;
  - bar 0.1 kg;
  - handle 1 kg.
- Articulation root `/smart_walker/Geometry/base_link`.
- 8 revolute joints (4 steering, 4 wheel) and 6 fixed, including **`ft_sensor_joint`**
  (protruding bar → handle, `localPos0` = (−0.035, 0, 0)).
- Wheel centres at (±0.25, ±0.19) m. Wheel mesh radius **0.10 m** (the URDF says 0.09).
- Handle at x = −0.395 m behind `base_link`, **0.933 m above the ground plane** (the ground
  plane being the wheel bottoms). It is one bar, 0.615 m long.

## What the simulation variant changes

Only colliders:

- **Disabled:** 261 non-primitive colliders. There were 260 convex-decomposition meshes plus one
  collision-tagged Xform, and they had to be un-instanced first.
- **Added:** four **frictionless spheres** (r = 0.10 m) at the wheel centres, and a
  **0.751 × 0.50 × 0.24 m chassis box**.
- **Kept:** the converter's handle, pillar and sensor primitives. Every mass and joint is
  unchanged.

The frictionless spheres are deliberate. The walker plan models the base as ideal planar
velocity tracking, so the ground carries only vertical load.
